About us

A computer shop that grew into infrastructure.

CureComp Technology started where most IT businesses do — fixing machines and selling parts in Seremban. The difference is where it went next: managed networks, a data centre, and Malaysia's first third-party GPON ONT.

What we do, in plain terms

We serve and sell computers, parts, supplies, services and repair. That's the counter you can walk up to.

Behind it, we operate as a Managed Service Provider — supplying computers, laptops, tablets and mobiles, designing and running networks (PON/GPON, Layer 2, Layer 3, firewalls, site-to-site, VPN) and Wi-Fi access points, with 24/7 live support.

And behind that, we run cloud and connectivity as a data centre operator, digital infrastructure provider and telecom service provider — dedicated servers, colocation, VM/LXC and custom IP allocation.

Very few companies cover all three. Doing so is exactly why nothing gets lost between the hardware vendor, the network team and the host: they're the same people.

How we operate

The habits behind the work

Say it straight

If a repair isn't economical, we say so. If a spec is overkill, we say that too. Quotes are written down before work starts.

Document everything

Diagrams, configurations and credentials belong to you. No system we touch should be a mystery to the next person.

Answer the phone

24/7 live support on covered contracts means an engineer, not a queue position and a promise to call back tomorrow.

Prefer open standards

Standards-based networking and supported open firmware where it makes sense — so you're never trapped by a single vendor.

Design for the bad day

Backups, spares and failover are decided while everything is working — that's the only time you can think clearly about them.

Stay local

Seremban is home. Being nearby means we can be on-site when remote support genuinely isn't enough.

Coverage

Based in Seremban. Reachable from anywhere.

On-site across Negeri Sembilan and the surrounding region, with wider coverage for contracted clients — and remote support wherever you are.
