Questions

Cloud, answered

Why isn't there a price list?
Because a realistic quote depends on the hardware, the storage, how much bandwidth you actually pull and whether you need committed capacity. Tell us the workload and you'll get a firm figure rather than a headline price that changes at checkout.
What does "shared, burstable" mean for my traffic?
Your port is 1 Gbps and can burst into shared capacity up to 10 Gbps when it's available — so short spikes don't throttle you. If your workload needs guaranteed throughput around the clock, ask about a committed bandwidth plan.
Can I bring my own IP space?
Yes. We support custom allocations and can work with your existing space and announcements, including reverse DNS delegation.
Can I move existing servers into colocation?
Yes — we'll plan the migration window, help with transport and racking, and get you back online with addressing and routing prepared in advance.
Do you manage the operating system too?
You can run it yourself, or add OS-level management to a managed IT contract — patching, monitoring, backups and 24/7 cover.
