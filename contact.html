Quick answers

Before you write

How do I get a price for managed IT or cloud?
Both are quoted against your requirement rather than sold from a price list. Give us the shape of it — sites, users, devices, workloads, uptime needs — and you'll get a written quotation.
What should I include for a hardware quote?
Model numbers or a parts list if you have one; otherwise describe what the machine is for, how many you need and roughly what budget you're working to.
What should I include for a PON Stick enquiry?
Your ISP and plan, and the router you intend to use. That's usually enough for us to confirm whether it's a good fit for your line.
Can you come to us?
Yes — on-site across Negeri Sembilan and surrounding areas, and further afield for contracted clients. Mention the location in your message.
