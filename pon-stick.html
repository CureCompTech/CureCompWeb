Why a PON Stick

Stop being the last to know

With an operator-managed ONT, the link is a black box: it either works or you file a ticket and wait. The PON Stick puts the instrumentation on your side of the fibre.

Manage and monitor it yourself

Optical power, temperature, PON state, uptime and alarms — read from your own side, so the numbers in your SLA are numbers you can actually check.

Peace of mind, not blind trust

No more unattended equipment quietly managed by someone else. You know the state of your own link at any moment, without asking permission.

Recovery in minutes

Swap in a spare stick, re-plug the fibre and you're back. No waiting for a truck roll just to replace a failed box on your wall.

Faster, more stable, lower latency

One less device, one less hop, one less NAT layer between your router and the fibre — a cleaner path with fewer things to go wrong.

Clean division of responsibility

The ISP technician does their part up to the fibre. You simply re-plug it into your router. Troubleshooting stops being an argument.

Less clutter, less power

An SFP-sized module in your router replaces a separate powered box, its adaptor and its patch cable. One device instead of three.

Questions

PON Stick, answered

What exactly is a PON Stick?
It's an ONT built into an SFP module. Instead of a separate powered box from your ISP, the fibre terminates in a module seated in your own router — so the router itself becomes the point where your service arrives.
Will it work on my ISP?
It supports the major Malaysian operators — TM Unifi, Maxis, TIME and PERFECT. Provisioning details vary by operator and plan, so send us your service details and we'll confirm before you buy.
Do I need a specific router?
You need a router with a suitable SFP cage and firmware that will work with an SFP ONT. Tell us what you're running — or what you're thinking of buying — and we'll tell you honestly whether it's a good match.
What happens when there's a fault on the line?
The fibre, splitter and OLT stay with the ISP, so their technician handles line-side faults exactly as before. When they're done you re-plug the fibre into your router and you're back — no waiting for equipment to be re-installed.
What is the NIJIKA Firmware SDK?
It's the firmware and development kit behind the Anime4000 GPON Stick. CureComp is an official supplier and reseller of both, which means the units we sell are genuine and supported rather than unknown-origin stock.
Can you deploy and support it for us?
Yes. PON Stick deployment can be rolled into a managed IT contract, including monitoring, spare units and 24/7 support on covered agreements.
Should I check with my ISP first?
If your service agreement has terms about the equipment on your line, review them — some operators have their own rules about third-party ONTs. We'll be straight with you about what's involved for your particular service.
