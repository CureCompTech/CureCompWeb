How we work

Straightforward from first call to steady state

  1. Listen

    A short call or site visit. We map what you have, what breaks, and what it costs you when it does.

  2. Scope & quote

    A written proposal with the hardware, the licences, the service levels and the price. No surprises later.

  3. Deploy

    Staged, tested and cut over out of hours where we can. You get the documentation and the credentials.

    Monitoring, patching, spares and a real human on the other end — 24/7 on covered contracts.
