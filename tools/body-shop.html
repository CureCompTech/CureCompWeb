How a repair runs

  1. Drop off or call out

    Bring the unit to Seremban Plaza, or book an on-site visit for fixed equipment.

  2. Diagnosis

    We test, identify the fault and check parts availability.

  3. Written quote

    You approve the cost and turnaround before we touch anything.

  4. Repair & test

    Fixed, stress-tested, cleaned, and your data verified intact.

  5. Handover

    Collected or delivered, with a note of what was replaced and any warranty on the work.
