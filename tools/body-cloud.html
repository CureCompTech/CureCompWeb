Beyond the shared pool

Pure 10 Gbps. Dedicated. Local and international.

Not burstable, not shared, not best-effort. A full 10 Gbps committed to you alone, on both domestic and international paths — because some workloads cannot be told to wait their turn.

We connect your server directly to Tier 1 transit — carriers such as NTT for international reach — and peer you locally at MyIX, the Malaysian Internet Exchange, so domestic traffic stays domestic instead of hairpinning through Singapore.

Our BGP policy is then written specifically for your prefix. Not a default route shared with everyone else on the platform: a path we pave for you, tuned for where your users and your data actually are.
